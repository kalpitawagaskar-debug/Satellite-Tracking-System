## Rules for the report and the demo
- Anything simulated must say so on screen: orbit (DEMO ORBIT), waterfall, SSTV image, dispatch network, health limits.
- Health limits are examples, not validated for a PocketQube.
- Routing is classical shortest-path (Yen's k-best), not machine learning.
- Credit data sources in the README and on the About/credits screen.
